Analyze your own parts

  1. Download a template. Start from the blank one, or from the 150-part sample to see real-looking data.
  2. Fill one row per purchased part with 26 weeks of demand, oldest week first.
  3. Save as CSV (in Excel: CSV UTF-8, comma delimited) and click Upload CSV.
Download 150-part sample

Required columns

part_numberUnique part ID
unit_cost_usdUnit cost in USD, greater than 0
lead_time_weeksSupplier lead time in weeks
moqMinimum order quantity, in units
current_programExactly one of VMI, CRP, Kanban, MRP
on_hand_qtyInventory on hand today, in units
w01 … w26Weekly demand in units, w01 = oldest, w26 = most recent. Use 0 for no demand.

Recommended columns

description, commodity, manufacturer, supplier. They drive the filters, the supplier view, the negotiation list and the rollout waves.

What happens with bad rows

Parts with a missing lead time, zero cost or no demand in 26 weeks are not dropped silently: they are listed in Data integrity and kept out of the analysis until fixed. An MOQ above 8 weeks of demand is flagged as a warning but still analyzed.
